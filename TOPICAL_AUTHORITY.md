# Topical Authority — gazebo.tukang.co.id

## Role and boundary

gazebo.tukang.co.id should become a practical Indonesian-language guide for planning, specifying, procuring, building, accepting, and maintaining outdoor gazebos. Editorial pages support the site's commercial routes; they do not reproduce “jasa pembuatan gazebo + kota” pages, replace `/services/`, or compete with the existing `/gazebo-bambu/`, `/gazebo-beton/`, `/gazebo-besi/`, `/gazebo-kayu/`, and `/rumah-kayu/` product landing pages.

Gazebos are exposed structures, so structural capacity, foundations, wind and rain, soil, connections, fire, electrical work, accessibility, permits, and worksite safety are evidence gates. Authors must use current Indonesian regulations and authoritative standards where applicable, project drawings and calculations, site measurements, manufacturer documentation for exact products, material certificates, and recorded inspections or tests. Structural, geotechnical, electrical, fire-safety, arboricultural, and legal judgments require suitably qualified specialists. Never invent loads, dimensions, material strength, service life, prices, permit duties, warranty terms, or test results.

## Context used

- `sitemap.xml` lists 137 URLs; `sitemap-complete.xml` lists 129, all of which are contained in the larger file.
- The larger sitemap contains 100 geographic service URLs, nine core/product routes, 12 category/archive entries, 12 date/archive entries, two feeds, one author path, and one login path. Four archive entries are query-parameter variants, yielding 133 unique normalized paths.
- The 129 tracked HTML routes correspond to 100 geographic service pages, 20 archive/pagination pages, and nine core/product pages.
- Homepage navigation and `/services/` present five commercial product families: gazebo bambu, gazebo beton, gazebo besi, gazebo kayu, and rumah kayu. The house route remains a separate commercial offer and is outside the gazebo editorial catalog.
- Homepage claims such as high quality, diversified design, long-term investment, or material sustainability were treated as promotional hypotheses, not evidence.

## Ignored template noise

Geographic keyword swaps, category/date pagination, feeds, author/login paths, query-parameter duplicates, repeated navigation/footer copy, repeated bodies, and metadata were excluded from topic discovery. A location name does not justify a separate editorial brief. Existing material and service landing pages own transactional browsing and quotation intent; editorial material guides must teach specification and verification rather than restating sales copy.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and the two or three sibling guides that complete the same reader decision. Cross-topic links follow the project lifecycle: GZB-01 brief → GZB-02/GZB-03 design → GZB-04–GZB-11 technical evidence → GZB-12 procurement → GZB-13 delivery → GZB-14 operation, with GZB-15 as the document and authority gate throughout. Material-selection articles may link to the matching existing product route only when the reader is ready for commercial exploration. No article links to city pages merely to vary anchors, and no house-focused editorial cluster is created from `/rumah-kayu/`.
